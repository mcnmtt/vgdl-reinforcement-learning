BasicGame
    SpriteSet
        Avatar > Player controlled character aiming/shooting abilities
        BluePad > Stationary blue objects remaining unchanged during gameplay
        Bullet > Projectile fired by Avatar; affected by gravity & moves quickly; single instance allowed
        Wall > Movement barriers destroying Bullets upon collision
    LevelMapping
        0 > BluePad(s), depending on number desired within level design
    InteractionSet
        Bullet Wall > KillBullet
        Wall Bullet > KillWall
        Bullet BluePad > KillBullet
        Avatar tryMoveIntoWall > StepBackward
        Avatar tryMovePastScreenEdge > StepBackward
        Bullet reachScreenEdge > KillBullet
    TerminationSet
        WinCondition = AllBluePadsDestroyed()
        LoseCondition = AvatarDestruction()
