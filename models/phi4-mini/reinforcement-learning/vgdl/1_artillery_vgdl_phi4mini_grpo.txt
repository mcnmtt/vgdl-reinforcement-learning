BasicGame
    SpriteSet
        Avatar > Player controlled character aiming/shooting abilities
        BluePad > Stationary blue objects
        Bullet > Projectile fired by Avatar; affected by gravity & fast-moving
        Wall > Movement-blocking barriers destroying Bullets upon collision
    LevelMapping
        0 > BluePad
    InteractionSet
        Bullet Wall > killBullet
        Wall Bullet > killWall
        Bullet BluePad > killBullet
        Avatar tryMoveIntoWall > stepBackward
        Avatar tryMovePastScreenEdge > stepBackward
        Bullet reachScreenEdge > killBullet
    TerminationSet
        WinCondition = SpriteCounter stype=BluePad limit=0 win=True
        LoseCondition = SpriteCounter stype=Avatar limit=0 win=False
